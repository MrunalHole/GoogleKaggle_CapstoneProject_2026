# Lucent: Parkinson's Screening & Education Platform
## YouTube Video Script & Walkthrough Guide
**Target Duration:** ~4.5 to 5 minutes

---

### Section 1: Introduction & The Problem (0:00 - 0:45)

**Visuals & Actions:**
1. **[0:00]** Start on the homepage/landing page of Lucent, showing the sleek, modern design.
2. **[0:15]** Briefly scroll down to show the mission statement or overview section.
3. **[0:30]** Navigate towards the "Education" or "About" section.

**Audio / Script:**
"Hello everyone, and welcome to our Google and Kaggle Capstone Project submission: **Lucent**. Parkinson’s Disease is a progressive nervous system disorder, and one of the biggest challenges in treating it is the difficulty of early diagnosis. Often, by the time motor symptoms become obvious, significant neurological damage has already occurred. However, subtle changes in voice—like hypophonia and monotone speech—can be very early indicators. We built Lucent to provide an accessible, non-invasive voice screening tool combined with a rich educational platform to empower patients and their families."

---

### Section 2: Educational Tools (0:45 - 1:45)

**Visuals & Actions:**
1. **[0:45]** Open the **3D Brain Explorer**. Click and drag the brain to show that it is fully interactive. Highlight specific regions like the substantia nigra and basal ganglia. 
2. **[1:15]** Switch to the **Disease Stage Simulator**. Slowly drag the slider across the five Hoehn & Yahr stages. 
3. **[1:30]** Point out the animated dopamine-level visualization changing as you move the slider.

**Audio / Script:**
"Education is the first step towards empowerment. Here on the frontend, built with React and Vite, we developed an interactive 3D brain explorer using React Three Fiber. Users can interact with the brain to understand exactly which regions are affected by Parkinson's, such as the substantia nigra. We also included a Disease Stage Simulator. As I move this slider through the five Hoehn & Yahr stages, users can visually understand the progression of symptoms and watch the animated dopamine levels deplete, making complex medical concepts much easier to grasp."

---

### Section 3: The Voice Screening Tool (1:45 - 3:15)

**Visuals & Actions:**
1. **[1:45]** Navigate to the **Voice Screening** page.
2. **[2:00]** Click the "Record" button, speak a brief phrase, and stop. (Alternatively, upload a pre-recorded audio file). 
3. **[2:15]** Wait a moment while the "Analyzing..." state is shown. 
4. **[2:30]** Show the results page. Hover over the likelihood score and the feature importances (like Jitter, Shimmer). 
5. **[2:50]** Highlight the plain-English clinical explanation provided underneath the score.

**Audio / Script:**
"The core of Lucent is our Voice Screening tool. Users can upload a voice clip or record directly in the browser. Once submitted, our FastAPI backend goes to work. It uses `ffmpeg` and Praat to extract real acoustic biomarkers. Then, our machine learning pipeline—utilizing Random Forest and SVM models trained on the UCI Parkinson's dataset—analyzes the features. 
Here we get a likelihood score and a breakdown of feature importances, showing exactly what the model noticed. But data alone can be scary. To solve this, we integrated Google Gemini AI. Gemini interprets these complex biomarkers and generates a structured, empathetic, plain-English clinical explanation, giving users context rather than just a cold number. Note that we clearly disclose this is a screening tool, not a medical diagnosis."

---

### Section 4: Personal Dashboard & Ask Lucent (3:15 - 3:45)

**Visuals & Actions:**
1. **[3:15]** Navigate to the **Personal Dashboard**. Show the symptom tracking charts and medication reminders.
2. **[3:30]** Open the **Ask Lucent** AI Assistant chat window. Type a quick question like: "What are the early signs of Parkinson's?" and show the AI's response.

**Audio / Script:**
"To help users manage their health over time, we built a Personal Dashboard. Here, users can track their symptoms—visualized beautifully with Recharts—and manage medication reminders. Past screening sessions are persisted securely in our PostgreSQL database. Furthermore, if users have questions at any time, they can chat with 'Ask Lucent', our dedicated Gemini-powered AI assistant that answers questions in a safe, easy-to-understand manner."

---

### Section 5: The Standalone ADK Agent (3:45 - 4:30)

**Visuals & Actions:**
1. **[3:45]** Switch away from the browser to a terminal window or IDE.
2. **[4:00]** Briefly show the `parkinsons-detector` agent code or run a quick command via `agents-cli playground` to show the agent processing a file. 
3. **[4:15]** Show the output diagnostic report markdown generated by the agent.

**Audio / Script:**
"Beyond the web platform, we also pushed the boundaries of AI orchestration by building a standalone clinical detection agent using the Google Agent Development Kit, or ADK. This ReAct agent can autonomously orchestrate dynamic ML models, enforce physiological data guardrails, and generate the same clinical explanations. It acts as a powerful backend microservice and demonstrates how easily this logic can be deployed as an autonomous medical screening assistant in enterprise environments."

We've built a holistic platform that doesn't just analyze voice data, but educates, supports, and empowers individuals.
